# CHEK2 Daily Report — 2026-09-25

## TL;DR
One new publication today: a Current Oncology review of non-BRCA hereditary breast/ovarian cancer genes that restates the established moderate-penetrance profile of CHEK2 (roughly two-fold or lower excess cancer risk) and notes the clinically important exception of biallelic carriers. Nothing that changes the baseline risk estimates for c.1100delC, Ile157Thr, or Ser428Phe carriers. No new preprints or trial updates of direct CHEK2 relevance.

## New Research Publications

**Breast-Ovarian Hereditary Cancer Syndrome: Beyond BRCA1 and BRCA2** (PMID 42782902)
*Current Oncology, 2026 Sep 14 — review*

This review surveys the growing list of hereditary breast/ovarian cancer genes beyond BRCA1/2, covering PALB2, RAD51C/D, BRIP1, CHEK2, ATM, and others. On CHEK2 specifically, it groups CHEK2 with ATM, BLM, and NBN as genes whose pathogenic variants confer "only a two-fold or even lower excess of cancer risk" — consistent with the established moderate-penetrance baseline (truncating variants OR ~2–2.7; missense Ile157Thr/Ser428Phe OR ~1.3). It also flags the rare but important scenario where panel testing uncovers biallelic germline inactivation of these genes, producing severe syndromic phenotypes requiring intensive management. Nothing in the review alters existing guidance for CHEK2 carriers; its value is in reinforcing that CHEK2's risk profile remains moderate and that extended panel testing is becoming the prevailing diagnostic approach.
