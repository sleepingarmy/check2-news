## New Research Publications

**Understanding CHEK2's role in breast cancer by analyzing breast cancers with and without CHEK2-specific genomic profiles**
*Scientific Reports — published 18 September 2026 — Qu, Smid, Martens, Hollestelle (Erasmus MC Cancer Institute, Rotterdam; Amsterdam UMC)*

Researchers analyzed structural variant (SV) size distributions in 926 primary and metastatic breast cancers and built a method to identify ER-positive tumors that *lack* a CHEK2 mutation but still show the CHEK2-specific genomic profile. Duplications in both CHEK2-mutant and "CHEK2-like" ER+ tumors clustered mainly on chromosomes 11 and 17, but no CHEK2-specific driver genes or regions emerged — CHEK2-like samples had significantly more structural variants yet fewer point mutations. The takeaway: CHEK2-mutant cancers appear to select the same drivers as other breast cancers but maintain genome stability differently, refining (not overturning) our mechanistic understanding of how CHEK2 loss drives ER+ breast cancer. No implications for carrier risk estimates.
Link: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-67158-w (DOI: 10.1038/s41598-026-67158-w)
