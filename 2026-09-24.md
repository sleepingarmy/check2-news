# CHEK2 Daily Report — 2026-09-24

## TL;DR
No new CHEK2 research papers or preprints today. Two CHEK2-relevant clinical trials had registry updates in the past week: the TAPUR precision-medicine study (which includes a CHEK2 cohort) and a trial testing high-dose testosterone in prostate cancers with ATM, CDK12, or CHEK2 alterations. Neither update changes anything for carriers — both studies remain open and recruiting.

## Clinical Trials Updates

**TAPUR: Testing the Use of FDA-Approved Drugs That Target a Specific Abnormality in a Tumor Gene in People With Advanced Stage Cancer** (NCT02693535)
*Registry record updated 22 September 2026 — status: Recruiting (ASCO, multi-site US)*

TAPUR is ASCO's long-running "basket" study that matches advanced-cancer patients to approved targeted drugs based on their tumor's genomic profile, including a cohort for CHEK2-mutated tumors treated with PARP inhibition. The record was refreshed this week with no change to its recruiting status; no new results were posted. Relevant because it remains one of the few active trials explicitly enrolling patients whose tumors carry CHEK2 alterations.
Link: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02693535

**High Dose Testosterone for ATM, CDK12 or CHEK2 Altered Prostate Cancers** (NCT05011383)
*Registry record updated 17 September 2026 — status: Recruiting (Johns Hopkins)*

This trial tests whether DNA-repair deficiency — including CHEK2 alterations — predicts response to intermittent high-dose (bipolar androgen) testosterone therapy in metastatic prostate cancer. The hypothesis is that genetically unstable, repair-deficient tumors are selectively vulnerable to this approach. Still recruiting; the recent update is administrative, with no results posted. Of interest to male CHEK2 carriers given the established ~2x prostate cancer risk association.
Link: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05011383
